About Dr. Akash Mahore

A Doctor. A Builder.
A Visionary.

I'm Dr. Akash Mahore — an ophthalmologist and founder of Mahalakshmi Netralaya, Khamgaon. My practice brings together clinical precision, patient empowerment, and bold innovation in eye care.

I've authored four books on eye health, developed clinical tools, and am building a comprehensive vision care ecosystem for the digital age.

My mission: to be the doctor who not only treats your eyes — but empowers you to protect them for life.
